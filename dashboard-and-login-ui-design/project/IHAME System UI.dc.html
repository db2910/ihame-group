1IHAME — login + manager dashboard, three directions

Assumptions: six users, three hardcoded roles; manager gets a persistent sidebar, freight/shop staff get a top bar. Balanced density. Desktop shown at 1360; each direction also has two 390px phone screens — login and the manager's bulk status update, which the spec calls out as needing to work well on a phone. Currency toggle sits in the page header — figures are shown in their native currency with the toggle switching the display base. Logo is a placeholder wordmark until you send the real mark. Direction differences are visual + structural, not content: all three carry the same six dashboard signals with awaiting review, outstanding balances and low stock weighted heaviest.

1aOperations — sober, by the book. Light surfaces, dark sidebar, tables do the work.
